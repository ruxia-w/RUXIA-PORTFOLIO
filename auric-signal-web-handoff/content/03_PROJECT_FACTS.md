## Product status

AURIC SIGNAL is a demonstration app. Portfolio values are static sample data. The supplied Privacy & Data screen states that nothing is transmitted to a broker or exchange. Simulation and rebalancing flows preview possible outcomes and save drafts; they do not execute trades or change real balances.

## Design framing — not validated facts

- Self-directed investors may see what changed without understanding why it matters to their own portfolio.
- Evidence, portfolio context, and scenario comparison may support more deliberate evaluation.
- Separating observed data from AI interpretation may make system boundaries easier to understand.
- Users may benefit from retaining explicit control over whether to save or act on a plan.

Use “may,” “could,” “the concept explores,” and “the design intends” for these statements.

## Target audience — design hypothesis

Self-directed retail investors who manage diversified assets and can read basic investment information, but need clearer support when interpreting market movements, concentration exposure, and the trade-offs of possible portfolio changes.
