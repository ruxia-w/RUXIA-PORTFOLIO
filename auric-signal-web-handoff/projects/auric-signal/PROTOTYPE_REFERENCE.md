## Prototype usage rules

- Use the prototype to verify sequence and terminology.
- Use supplied screenshots as the visual source of truth.
- Do not scrape or depend on the live prototype at build time.
- Do not embed the entire prototype as a substitute for the case study.
- External prototype links open with a clear accessible label.
- Do not claim every diagrammed route is implemented; the supplied screenshots define documented coverage.
